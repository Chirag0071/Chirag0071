<sub><b>Face-authenticated voting · Jun 2026 · Live on Render</b></sub>

Face auth on **128-d encodings** with **blink-based liveness detection** (Eye Aspect Ratio, 10 frames in 2 s, 8% min drop) to reject photo and screen spoofing.

- 👥 1-to-N duplicate-face detection at registration (≤ 0.6)
- 🔐 **Fernet/AES**-encrypted encodings, **0 raw photos stored**, 5-min lockout after 5 failed attempts
- 🕶️ Anonymous ballots with **0 voter-linking columns**, bcrypt + JWT, audit log
- ☁️ **37 commits**, managed MySQL on Aiven

`Python` `dlib` `OpenCV` `FastAPI` `MySQL` `JWT`
